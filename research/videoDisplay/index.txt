.Talk at FrOSCon 2025
This research was motivated by the effort towards a first integration
of the Lumiera render engine; an overview of the results and parts of the code
were https://programm.froscon.org/2025/events/3316.html[presented in a talk]
at the https://froscon.org/[FrOSCon conference] 2025.
The video stream was recorded and archieved at
https://media.ccc.de/v/froscon2025-3316-getting_your_hands_dirty_with_video_output_on_linux_using_c[media.ccc.de]
